The Challenge

Traditional crowd counting fails in dense, overlapping scenes. Manual annotation is slow, error-prone, and doesn't scale to real-time needs.

  • Head detection fails at high density
  • No pixel-level spatial context
  • Cannot run at inference speed
✦

Our Solution

Density Map Regression via a deep CNN backbone — each pixel produces a density value, and summing the map gives the precise crowd count.

  • Pixel-level density estimation
  • End-to-end trainable pipeline
  • Real-time 30 FPS inference
